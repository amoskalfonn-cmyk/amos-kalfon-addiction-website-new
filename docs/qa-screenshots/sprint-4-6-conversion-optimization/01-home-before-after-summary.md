# Sprint 4.6 Home Before/After Summary

## Before
- The homepage already offered a calm first-step route to `start-here.html`.
- The Treatment Guidance route existed elsewhere, but it was not visible in the short first-step line near the homepage decision area.
- Visitors who were unsure whether they needed general orientation or treatment-option guidance had to infer the next path.

## After
- The homepage first-step line now offers two explicit routes:
  - `start-here.html` for visitors asking "Where do I begin?"
  - `services/treatment-guidance.html` for visitors ready to understand treatment options.
- No hero, layout, navigation, metadata, schema, WhatsApp link, JavaScript or branding changes were made.
- The change supports qualified enquiries by helping visitors self-select the right next step before contact.
