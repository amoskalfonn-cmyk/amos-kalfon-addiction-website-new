# CTA and Conversion Standard

Sprint 4.6 creates a calm conversion standard for the website. The goal is qualified enquiry quality, not pressure or click volume.

## Core Principles

- Use one primary next step per section.
- Use secondary links only when they clarify a different visitor intent.
- Prefer educational routing before direct contact when the visitor is likely unsure.
- Keep contact available, but avoid repeated large contact blocks on the same page.
- Do not use fear, urgency, scarcity, countdowns, popups, auto-opening WhatsApp or guilt-based copy.
- Do not imply diagnosis, treatment, recovery outcomes, emergency response or medical assessment.
- Do not ask visitors to send sensitive medical, legal or personal detail in forms.

## Approved CTA Types

| Type | Preferred Hebrew Wording | Use Case |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary routing | מאיפה מתחילים? | Visitor is unsure which page or journey fits. |
| Treatment decision | להבין אפשרויות טיפול | Visitor is comparing help, frameworks or next steps. |
| Trust / background | לקרוא על הגישה | Visitor needs confidence before contact. |
| Direct enquiry | לקיים שיחה ראשונית | Visitor is ready to contact without pressure. |
| Contact | יצירת קשר | Clear general contact action. |
| Family | מידע לבני משפחה | Visitor is worried about another person. |
| Professional | מידע לאנשי מקצוע | Visitor is a referrer or professional reader. |

## Avoided CTA Wording

- התחילו עכשיו
- שנו את החיים היום
- אל תחכו
- קבלו טיפול מיד
- הדרך להחלמה מתחילה כאן
- נשארו מקומות אחרונים

## Page-Level Hierarchy

| Page Type | Primary CTA | Secondary Path | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home | מאיפה מתחילים? | להבין אפשרויות טיפול | Keep WhatsApp available but do not make every section a contact push. |
| Start Here | Choose route | לקיים שיחה ראשונית | Contact appears once, after routing and safety wording. |
| Treatment Guidance | Questionnaire / guidance explanation | Contact after useful context | Do not redesign the questionnaire. |
| Contact | שליחת פנייה ראשונית | Start Here / Treatment Guidance / For Professionals | Help visitors self-qualify without mandatory screening. |
| Knowledge Center | Start Here / Treatment Guidance | Topic reading | Avoid sending unsure readers straight to contact. |
| For Professionals | יצירת קשר מקצועית | Trust Center | Keep boundaries explicit. |
| Pillar Articles | Relevant article journey | Treatment Guidance | Avoid generic repeated CTAs. |
| Family Articles | Family guidance | Treatment Guidance | Keep coercion and safety boundaries clear. |

## Ethical Conversion Rules

- CTA purpose must be understandable without colour.
- CTA labels should describe the action or destination.
- WhatsApp links remain optional and must not auto-open.
- Forms must remain simple and must not collect unnecessary sensitive detail.
- Emergency wording should be clear but not dominate non-emergency pages.
- Trust should be built before direct contact is requested.

## Performance Rule

Conversion improvements should use existing HTML and CSS components. Do not add third-party libraries, new fonts, heavy media, analytics changes, new scripts or extra network requests for CTA changes.
